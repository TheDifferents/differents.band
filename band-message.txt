Dance floor list - where we're at

I went back through the book against what's actually got people up.
These 15 are the ones that have genuinely worked, split by tempo:

SLOW (under 110)
  Superstition - Stevie Wonder - 100 BPM
  9 to 5 - Dolly Parton - 103 BPM
  Walking On Sunshine - Katrina and the Waves - 108 BPM
  Low Rider - War - 140 BPM (counts 140, but it sits like a 70 jam)

MEDIUM (110-140)
  Don't You Forget About Me - Simple Minds - 112 BPM
  I Will Survive - Gloria Gaynor - 117 BPM
  Billie Jean - Michael Jackson - 117 BPM
  Just What I Needed - The Cars - 129 BPM
  Jumpin' Jack Flash - The Rolling Stones - 137 BPM

FAST (140+)
  Burning Love - Elvis Presley - 144 BPM
  Mr. Brightside - The Killers - 148 BPM
  Crazy Little Thing Called Love - Queen - 152 BPM
  What I Like About You - The Romantics - 160 BPM
  Valerie - The Zutons - 169 BPM
  Time Warp - The Rocky Horror Picture Show - 176 BPM

That's the whole proven dance set - 15 songs, a bit under an hour.
Mr. Brightside is the newest and it worked both nights we tried it.

FAST (140+)

  You Make My Dreams (Come True) - Daryl Hall & John Oates - 167 BPM
  Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go - Wham! - 168 BPM
  Take on Me - a-ha - 169 BPM

The pattern in what works for us is pretty clear: big singable choruses,
not riffs and grooves. Almost everything we dropped was a groove record.
On that basis Play That Funky Music and Maneater are the two riskiest here.
